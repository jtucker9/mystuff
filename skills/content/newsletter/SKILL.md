Output on activation: `Newsletter — Writing your newsletter edition...`

## Instructions

### Step 1: Gather Inputs

Collect before writing. Skip known values, ask for unknowns:

| Input | Why It Matters |
|-------|---------------|
| Audience | Drives tone, depth, reference points |
| Topic/theme | Scopes the edition |
| Newsletter name | Branding in subject + footer |
| Content type | Determines structure (see decision below) |
| Frequency | Daily/weekly/biweekly/monthly — affects depth and length |
| Brand voice | Casual / professional / witty / authoritative |
| Platform | ConvertKit, Beehiiv, Substack, Mailchimp, SendGrid |
| Monetization | Sponsored / affiliate / paid tier / none |

**Content type decision:**
- **Curated** — 5-7 links with YOUR commentary (2-3 sentences each). Best for: daily/weekly news roundups.
- **Original** — Single essay or tutorial (500-800 words). Best for: thought leadership, deep expertise.
- **Hybrid** — Original main piece + curated secondary section. Best for: weekly cadence, balanced value.
- **Digest** — Bullet-point summaries of your own content from the week. Best for: creators with multiple channels.

**Gate:** Do not proceed without audience, topic, and content type confirmed.

### Step 2: Write Subject Line and Preview Text

Generate 3 subject line options (50 chars max), ranked by expected performance.

**Subject line formulas (pick the best fit):**
- Number + benefit: "5 tools that saved me 10 hours this week"
- Question: "Are you making this hiring mistake?"
- How-to: "How to write copy that converts (template inside)"
- Contrarian: "Why I stopped using React"
- Urgency: "This deal expires Friday" — use sparingly
- Personal: "I almost quit last week. Here's why I didn't."
- News hook: "Google just changed SEO forever"

**Preview text (90 chars):** Complements subject, never repeats it. Adds context or teases secondary content.

**Gate:** Subject lines must be under 50 chars. Preview must not duplicate subject.

### Step 3: Write Newsletter Body

**Section structure framework:**

1. **Personal opener** (2-3 sentences) — Anecdote, observation, or timely hook connecting to main topic. Builds connection before value delivery.
2. **Main content** — Format depends on content type decision from Step 1. One main idea per edition.
3. **Secondary content** (optional) — Quick hits (3-5 bullets), tool of the week, reader Q&A, or curated quote with your take.
4. **CTA** — One clear action: reply, share, check out resource, or upgrade. Never multiple competing CTAs.
5. **Footer** — Referral link + unsubscribe. Required.

**Writing rules:**
- Conversational tone — like emailing a smart friend
- Subheadings every 150-200 words for scannability
- Bold key phrases — readers scan before reading
- Paragraphs: 2-3 sentences max
- At least one actionable takeaway per edition

**CTA placement decision:** Primary CTA goes after main content. If reply-based CTA, place at end of opener instead (higher engagement when personal context is fresh). Never put CTA inside sponsored section.

**Curation rules (curated/hybrid types only):**
- Add YOUR perspective — readers follow you, not sources
- 5-7 items max (more feels like a dump)
- Mix sources — don't over-index on one publication
- Include at least one contrarian or surprising piece

**Gate:** Body must have opener + main content + CTA minimum. One idea per edition.

### Step 4: Sponsorship Placement

Skip if no monetization. Otherwise:

| Slot | Position | Format |
|------|----------|--------|
| Header sponsor | Top, before content | Logo + 1-line mention |
| Mid-content ad | Between sections | 2-3 sentences + link |
| Classified | Bottom, after content | 1-2 sentences + link |

Rules: Label clearly. Write in YOUR voice. Max 1 sponsor per edition (2 if header + classified). Never sponsor products you wouldn't recommend. Track CTR for sponsor reporting.

### Step 5: Growth Strategy and Analytics

**Growth strategy types (pick 1-2 for this month):**

| Tactic | Effort | Impact |
|--------|--------|--------|
| Referral program | Medium | High — tiered rewards (1/3/5/10/25 referrals) |
| Cross-promotion | Low | Medium — swap recs with similar newsletters |
| Lead magnet | Medium | High — free resource for subscribing |
| Social sharing | Low | Medium — share snippets as threads/posts |
| SEO archive | Medium | High long-term — publish past editions as blog posts |
| Guest posts | High | Medium — write for other newsletters |

**Engagement metrics and benchmarks:**

| Metric | Target | Industry Avg | Action if Below |
|--------|--------|-------------|-----------------|
| Open rate | 40-50% | 30-35% | Test subject lines, clean list |
| Click rate | 5-8% | 2-4% | Improve CTAs, reduce link count |
| Unsubscribe | < 0.3% | 0.2-0.5% | Check frequency, content relevance |
| Reply rate | 1-3% | < 1% | Ask more questions, be personal |
| Growth rate | 5-10%/mo | 2-5%/mo | Increase promotion, add lead magnet |

**Personalization approach:** Segment by engagement level (active/passive/cold). Remove unengaged (no opens in 90 days) quarterly. Send re-engagement before removing. Use double opt-in.

**Frequency/cadence decision:** Daily = short curated (high churn risk). Weekly = hybrid or original (sweet spot). Biweekly = deep original (lower growth). Monthly = comprehensive digest (high unsubscribe risk from forgetting). Match cadence to content type and capacity.

### Step 6: Deliver Output

Present: edition metadata, 3 ranked subject lines with preview text, full newsletter body, growth plan with this month's focus tactic, analytics targets.

**Gate:** Every edition must include subject lines, body with all required sections, and at least one growth tactic.

## Examples

**Curated tech newsletter (weekly):** 5 links with 2-3 sentence commentary each, personal opener about a shipping experience, "tool of the week" secondary, reply-CTA asking what readers shipped. Subject: "5 things worth your time this week" / Preview: "Including: the database trick nobody talks about."

**Original founder newsletter (biweekly):** 600-word essay on a specific lesson learned, no secondary section, share-CTA with referral program in footer, header sponsor slot. Subject: "The $40K mistake I made in Q1" / Preview: "And the framework that would have prevented it."

## Common Issues

- **Too many links / no focus** — One main idea per edition. Curated type gets 5-7 links max, all around a theme. Random link dumps have the lowest engagement.
- **Subject line too long** — 50 chars is the mobile visibility cutoff. If it gets truncated, open rates drop. Front-load the hook.
- **Inconsistent cadence** — Missing sends trains readers to ignore you. Pick a frequency you can sustain for 6 months. Weekly is safer than daily.

## Anti-Patterns

- Writing sponsor copy in corporate voice instead of yours
- Multiple competing CTAs in one edition
- Curating without adding your own perspective
- Skipping the personal opener (kills reply rates)
- Publishing without subject line A/B testing capability
- Growing list without engagement segmentation (vanity metrics)

## Escalation

- Newsletter platform migration (list export/import, DNS changes) — manual process, platform-specific
- Deliverability issues (landing in spam, blacklisted IP) — requires ESP support and domain authentication audit
- Revenue optimization beyond basic sponsorship — see pricing-strategy and sales-funnel skills
