## Instructions

### Step 1: Gather Inputs

Collect from user -- ask for anything missing. Do not assume silently.

| Input | Required | Default |
|-------|----------|---------|
| Topic | Yes | -- |
| Target audience | Yes | -- |
| Video length | No | 10-12 min (Short 5-7, Standard 10-12, Long 15-20, Deep Dive 25-40) |
| Channel niche | No | Derived from topic |
| Tone | No | Conversational, medium energy |
| CTA goal | No | Subscribe |
| A-roll format | No | Talking head |
| Brand constraints | No | None |

**Gate:** Do not proceed without topic + audience confirmed.

### Step 2: Hook Engineering (First 30 Seconds)

Select one primary hook type. Optionally layer a secondary.

**7 Hook Types:** Curiosity gap | Bold claim | Story cold open | Demonstration | "Don't do X" | Pattern interrupt | Question

**30-Second Structure (non-negotiable):**
- `[0:00-0:05]` HOOK -- stop the scroll, one sentence
- `[0:05-0:15]` CONTEXT -- why this matters, why now
- `[0:15-0:30]` PROMISE -- what the viewer gains; optionally open a loop

**Decision rules:** Tutorial/demo topics -> Demonstration or "Don't do X". Story/personal -> Story cold open. Opinion/controversy -> Bold claim or Question. Always hook before any branded intro.

**Gate:** Hook must fit in 30 seconds and contain all three beats (hook/context/promise).

### Step 3: Structure Selection

Choose structure, then plan chapters with timestamps, key points, energy level, and visual mode.

| Structure | Timing Skeleton | Best For |
|-----------|----------------|----------|
| **Tutorial** | [0:00 Hook] [0:30 Setup] [2:00-8:00 Steps] [8:00 Mistakes] [9:30 Recap] [10:30 CTA] | How-to, walkthroughs, builds |
| **Story-Driven** | [0:00 Cold open] [0:30 Rewind] [2:00 Act 1] [4:00 Act 2] [7:00 Act 3] [9:00 Reflect] [10:00 CTA] | Personal, career, documentary |
| **Listicle** | [0:00 Hook] [0:30-8:00 Items, strongest first + last] [9:00 Bonus] [10:00 CTA] | Tips, tools, mistakes, ranked |
| **Comparison** | [0:00 Hook] [0:30 Quick verdict] [1:30-7:30 Criteria] [8:30 Decision framework] [9:30 CTA] | Reviews, "vs" videos |
| **Day-in-the-Life** | [0:00 Highlight reel] [0:30 Morning] [2:00-7:00 Activity blocks] [8:30 Wrap] [10:00 CTA] | Lifestyle, productivity |
| **Deep Dive / Essay** | [0:00 Hook] [0:30 Background] [5:00-11:00 Core argument] [13:00 Counterpoints] [15:00 Implications] [17:00 CTA] | Essays, analysis, "why" |
| **Opinion / Commentary** | [0:00 State opinion] [1:30-5:30 Evidence] [7:00 Nuance] [8:30 Viewer implication] [9:30 CTA] | Hot takes, reactions, news |

**Energy planning:** Energy must rise and fall. Hook = high. Post-hook = drop to medium. Each chapter = brief spike then settle. Before key reveals = dip then spike. CTA = match or exceed hook energy. Flat energy (high or low) causes fatigue.

**Gate:** Chapter plan must have timestamps, durations, and energy levels before writing begins.

### Step 4: Script Writing

Write complete script using production notation.

**Notation system:**

`[SHOW: ...]` on-screen visual | `[CUT TO: ...]` angle/scene change | `[B-ROLL: ...]` supplementary footage | `[SCREEN RECORDING: ...]` screen capture | `[TEXT ON SCREEN: ...]` overlay/lower third | `[SFX: ...]` sound effect | `[MUSIC: ...]` music direction | `[PAUSE: Xs]` deliberate pause | `[ENERGY: ...]` delivery direction | `[AD-LIB: ...]` riff point | `(...)` stage direction

**Pacing by length:**

| Length | Word Count | WPM | Chapters |
|--------|-----------|-----|----------|
| 5-7 min | 750-1,050 | ~150 | 4-5 |
| 10-12 min | 1,500-1,800 | ~150 | 6-8 |
| 15-20 min | 2,250-3,000 | ~150 | 8-12 |
| 25-40 min | 3,750-6,000 | ~150 | 12-20 |

Heavy B-roll reduces spoken word count. Max sentence length: 25 words. Write spoken English (contractions, signposts, rhythm variation). Front-load each section's strongest line.

### Step 5: Retention Techniques

Layer these throughout the script:

- **Pattern interrupts:** Visual cut, B-roll insert, graphic pop, SFX, energy shift, direct address, physical movement. Frequency: every 20-30s in first 3 min, every 45-60s after. Never exceed 60s without one.
- **Open loops:** Plant 2-3 in the first 2 min. Resolve at different points (not all at the end). Every loop opened must be resolved.
- **"But first" transitions:** Convert section transitions (natural exit points) into curiosity hooks. "I'm going to show you X -- but first, you need to understand why Y."
- **Visual variety:** No single visual mode longer than 90s. Annotate mode changes in script.
- **Audience interaction:** 1 prompt every 3-4 min (poll, prediction, relatability check, challenge). Place after strongest insights.

**Gate:** Script must have open loop tracking (plant timestamp -> resolve timestamp) and pattern interrupt count logged.

### Step 6: CTA Integration

- **Subscribe prompt:** Between min 2-4. Tie to content promise, not generic ask. "I share stuff like this every Tuesday -- hit subscribe if you want more."
- **Mid-roll CTA (8+ min videos):** At natural midpoint between chapters. Max 8 seconds. Like ask tied to value just delivered.
- **End screen (last 20-30s):** Summarize in 1 sentence, point/gesture to card area, reference next video by topic. Never say goodbye before end screen. Hold for 15-20s.
- **Comment prompts:** 1-2 per video. Place after best insights. Types: opinion poll, experience share, debate starter, specific question.

### Step 7: SEO and Metadata

**Title formulas:** How I [Result] in [Time] | [Number] Mistakes [Consequence] | Why [Thing] Is [Unexpected] | [A] vs [B]: Honest Review | I Tried [X] for [Time] | Stop [Doing X] (Do This Instead) | [Topic] Explained in [Time] | What Nobody Tells You About [X]

**Title rules:** 50-70 chars. Front-load interesting word. Odd numbers outperform even. One caps word max. No clickbait that content can't deliver.

**Thumbnail text:** Max 4-5 words. Readable at mobile size. Contrasting colors. Do not duplicate title. Face with expression when possible. Formulas: outcome word ("FASTER"), number+result ("$47K"), question ("WORTH IT?"), versus ("A vs B"), emotion ("I QUIT"), warning ("DON'T").

**Description:** First 150 chars are search-visible. Always include timestamps (YouTube indexes them). 3-5 hashtags. 5-10 links max.

**Tags:** 10-15 total. Exact match (1-2) + broad (3-5) + long-tail (3-5) + related topics (2-3) + channel name (1).

Provide 2 thumbnail concepts with text, expression, color palette.

### Step 8: Output Assembly

Deliver: chapter plan table | full timestamped script with notation | SEO metadata (title, description, tags) | thumbnail concepts | retention notes (open loops, interrupt count, CTA placements) | production notes (B-roll shot list, screen recordings, graphics, music/SFX cues).

**Gate:** Verify word count aligns with target length at ~150 wpm before delivering.

## Examples

**Example 1 -- Tutorial request:**
Input: "YouTube script about setting up Docker for beginners, 10 min, tech niche"
Output: Tutorial structure. Demonstration hook showing finished container running. 6 chapters (hook, prerequisites, install, first container, common errors, next steps). Pattern interrupts via screen recording / talking head alternation. Subscribe CTA at min 3 after first working example. Title: "Docker in 10 Minutes -- Everything You Need to Start."

**Example 2 -- Opinion request:**
Input: "Script about why coding bootcamps are a scam, 12 min, controversial take"
Output: Opinion/Commentary structure. Bold claim hook with stat. 7 chapters building the argument with counterpoint at min 7. Open loop at 0:45 ("I actually went to one -- I'll show you what happened"). Comment prompt after strongest evidence. Title: "Why I Stopped Recommending Coding Bootcamps."

## Common Issues

| Issue | Fix |
|-------|-----|
| Script reads like a blog post | Rewrite with contractions, signposts ("Look...", "Here's the thing"), max 25-word sentences. Read aloud -- if you stumble, rewrite. |
| Retention cliff at 0:30 | Hook is weak or branded intro precedes it. Cut intro, strengthen context/promise beat. |
| Word count exceeds target length | Cut depth per point rather than cutting points. A 10-min video = 3-5 points, not 15. |

## Anti-Patterns

- **Blog-to-script conversion.** Written and spoken English are different registers. Short sentences, contractions, rhythm variation.
- **Best content saved for the end.** Most viewers leave before halfway. Front-load strongest material.
- **Flat energy throughout.** Engagement comes from contrast (peaks and valleys), not absolute energy level.
- **Generic CTAs.** "Like and subscribe" is invisible. Tie every CTA to specific value delivered.
- **Branded intro before hook.** Retention killer. Hook first, always.
- **Thumbnail duplicates title.** They're a team -- thumbnail adds emotion/visual the title cannot convey.
- **Unresolved open loops.** Every tease must pay off. Broken promises train viewers to ignore future hooks.
- **No pattern interrupts for 3+ min.** Causes retention cliff. Max 60s between interrupts.
